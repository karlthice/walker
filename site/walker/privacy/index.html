Walker Privacy Policy

Last updated 19 November 2026

Walker clears a fog from the map wherever you go. It is built so that everything it records stays on your iPhone.

Walker is made by Stratos ehf. (“we”). This policy explains what the app does with your information.

What Walker records

  • Your location. With your permission, Walker records where you go, including while the app is closed, so it can clear the fog along your path. The points are stored only in Walker’s own storage on your iPhone.
  • Your explored map and stats. The areas you have uncovered, and the totals worked out from them, are calculated and stored on your iPhone.

Your photos

If you turn on photos on the map, Walker reads where your photos were taken so it can show them as pins. Your photos are only read, never changed, copied elsewhere or uploaded. You can turn this off at any time, or remove Walker’s access in the iOS Settings app.

What leaves your iPhone

Nothing Walker records is sent to us or to anyone else. Walker has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.

The map itself comes from Apple Maps: to draw it, your iPhone downloads map images for the area you are viewing from Apple, as any app showing an Apple map does. Apple’s own privacy policy covers this. Country and city boundaries ship inside the app, so finding which country or city you are in never needs the internet.

Backups

If you back up your iPhone to iCloud or to a computer, Walker’s data is included in that backup, like other apps’ data, and is handled under your backup settings.

Deleting your data

Open the map, tap the gear button, and choose Delete All Data. This removes your tracks, the explored map and your stats from your iPhone. Deleting the app also deletes all of its data.

Children

Walker collects no personal information from anyone, including children.
